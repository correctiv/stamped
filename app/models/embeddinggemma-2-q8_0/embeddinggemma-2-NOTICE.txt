EmbeddingGemma 2
================

This product redistributes the EmbeddingGemma 2 model
(https://huggingface.co/google/embeddinggemma-2) as a GGUF quantization, taken from
the ggml-org/embeddinggemma-2-GGUF repository
(https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/embeddinggemma-2-GGUF).

The file embeddinggemma-2-Q8_0.gguf is the Q8_0 quantization of that model, pinned at
the revision 58faf90940886df2d62be555c8765d00dac362c5
(sha256 2188ac1deca4b77dffefd603c2776a9d76d9d74ec01841392982ebb840b09135).

shipped beside this notice as embeddinggemma-2-LICENSE.txt, and is also available at
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0.

The GGUF conversion is the work of the llama.cpp project
(https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp).
